About The Event

Join the Largest University Tech Carnival!

TechFest 2026 is the flagship annual technology festival organized by the Department of CSE. It brings together hundreds of tech enthusiasts, innovators, developers, and researchers from across the nation for two days of intense competition, learning, and collaboration.

Featuring hackathons, competitive programming, AI research showcases, and keynote speeches by global tech leaders, this event promises to empower students with hands-on experience and industry networking opportunities.
