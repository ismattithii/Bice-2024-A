University Inaugurates State-of-the-Art Artificial Intelligence & Innovation Hub

Published on August 25, 2026 | By Campus Desk

In a historic milestone for higher education, the Vice-Chancellor inaugurated the new Multidisciplinary AI Research Center today. Equipped with advanced GPU clusters and collaborative research labs, the hub aims to bridge the gap between academic innovation and industry solutions.

The facility will host international workshops, fund undergraduate innovation projects, and support cross-departmental research in Machine Learning, Robotics, and Data Science. Students and faculty members can register for access starting next week.
